# TA Pass-Off Quick Reference (Hybrid Competency)

Use this guide for consistent pass-off scoring in Unit 1 and Unit 2.

## Standard Timing

- Target duration: 5 to 10 minutes per student/team.
- Recommended flow:
  1. 1 minute context setup by student
  2. 3 to 6 minutes challenge prompts
  3. 1 to 2 minutes scoring and feedback

## Scoring Levels

Use one level per checkpoint:

1. Not Yet
2. Approaching
3. Meets
4. Exceeds

Use Meets as the default pass threshold.

## Fast Scoring Anchors

### Competency 1 (Traceability and Blueprinting)

- Not Yet:
  - Student cannot connect persona, story, workflow, IA, and wireframe coherently.
  - Rationale is vague or contradictory.
- Approaching:
  - Chain is partially complete but has one or more gaps.
  - Decisions are reasonable but weakly justified.
- Meets:
  - Full chain is coherent and defensible.
  - Scope and tradeoffs are explicit and aligned to MVP.
- Exceeds:
  - Student anticipates edge cases and explains impact of changes clearly.

### Competency 5 (Accessibility Awareness)

- Not Yet:
  - Accessibility claims are generic or missing.
- Approaching:
  - One accessibility idea appears, but implementation details are unclear.
- Meets:
  - Student identifies at least one concrete accessibility risk and mitigation.
  - Error handling uses text guidance, not color alone.
- Exceeds:
  - Student explains accessibility implications across multiple states and interactions.

### Competency 6 (AI Oversight)

- Not Yet:
  - Student cannot explain what AI produced or what was validated.
- Approaching:
  - Student can describe AI usage but provides limited critique.
- Meets:
  - Student identifies one AI output issue and explains a concrete correction.
- Exceeds:
  - Student explains validation method and residual risk.

## Prompt Script by Challenge

### Challenge A: MVP Defense

Ask:

1. What changed when a new feature request appeared?
2. Which item did you defer and why?
3. Show how one success criterion maps to persona and story.

### Challenge B: Workflow-to-IA Trace

Ask:

1. Walk one story from workflow step to sitemap location to wireframe state.
2. Show one alternate or error path and recovery.
3. Explain one label choice in user language.

### Challenge C: Wireframe Decision Defense

Ask:

1. Defend one design decision and one tradeoff.
2. Show one accessibility-aware error state.
3. Explain one AI-generated suggestion you revised.

## Common Failure Patterns

- Story and workflow mismatch.
- IA labels use team jargon instead of user language.
- Error states exist visually but lack clear text guidance.
- AI usage note is descriptive only and not evaluative.

## Feedback Sentence Starters

- "Your traceability is strongest from **_ to _**; strengthen the jump between **_ and _**."
- "To reach Meets, make one explicit scope boundary for \_\_\_."
- "Your accessibility note is a good start; add specific error text for \_\_\_."
- "Your AI reflection improves when you name exactly what was incorrect and what you changed."

## Retry Workflow

- If score is Not Yet or Approaching:
  1. Give one required fix and one optional improvement.
  2. Request a focused revision, not a full rewrite.
  3. Allow retake within the active unit window.
- Target feedback turnaround: within 48 hours.

## Format Parity Rules

Use equivalent scoring criteria across delivery mode:

1. Small-group live pass-off
2. Asynchronous video pass-off with short TA follow-up when needed

Do not lower or raise standards based on format.

## Calibration Tips

- Co-score the first 3 to 5 pass-offs with another TA/instructor.
- Compare level decisions and resolve disagreements quickly.
- Keep a short examples bank of Meets vs Approaching evidence.
