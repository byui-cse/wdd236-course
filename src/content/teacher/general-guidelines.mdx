## Class Time 

### Live Sections

Live sections of the course meet for 3 hours each week. Once you get into the flow of the course this is how I recommend you use that time:

- Instruction on new topics
- Contract review with peers
- Competency pass-offs with the instructor or TA

### Online Sections

In online sections I would recommend you offer a weekly lab that you record where you can address questions and topics from the week. You can also offer a live session for students to ask questions and get help with their projects. Pull the topics either from the weekly recommended path or from the questions students ask in the discussion forum. 

You will note that there is no normal grading load for the course. The only grading is the competency pass-offs. This means that you can spend your time helping students learn and grow rather than grading assignments.
