> ## Tips
>
> - Learn for understanding, not completion. Some topics will click quickly; others will take repeated review and comparison across sources.
> - Use multiple learning modes. If one source is unclear, switch formats (article, video, tutorial, AI conversation) and compare explanations.
> - Take notes in your own words. Add examples and questions so you can explain each term in class.

## Topics to Learn

Your goal this week is to learn the following terms and ideas. You can use any learning method that works for you: articles, videos, tutorials, AI chat, or a mix.

1. Course learning outcomes (how this unit supports them)
2. Usability heuristics
3. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
4. MoSCoW prioritization (Must/Should/Could/Won't)
5. Persona (what makes a useful persona)
6. User story format and purpose
7. Constrained project selection and scope
