## Topics to Learn

Your goal this week is to learn the following terms and ideas. You can use any learning method that works for you: articles, videos, tutorials, AI chat, or a mix.

1. User flow vs user journey
2. Information architecture (IA) vs sitemap
3. Wireframes (purpose, levels of fidelity, and process for creating them)
4. Acceptance criteria (what makes criteria testable: how do we know when we are done?)
5. Accessibility error identification (WCAG 3.3.1)
6. Workflow states (trigger, main path, alternate path, success state)
7. IA risk or uncertainty (how to identify and communicate one)
