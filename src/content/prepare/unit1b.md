> ## Tips
>
> - Keep your notes focused on decisions and tradeoffs, not just definitions.
> - Write one question for each resource you review to bring into team discussion.

## Reading

- Revisit the [WDD 236 Syllabus](../resources/syllabus) and identify which outcomes your project idea supports best.
- Read [User Flow Basics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-flows/).
- Read [Information Architecture Basics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-architecture-ia-sitemap/).
- Read [How to Create a Sitemap](https://www.uxpin.com/studio/blog/what-is-a-sitemap/).
- Read [How to Create Wireframes](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/wireframing).
- Read [Acceptance Criteria Explained](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/acceptance-criteria).
- Read [Understanding Success Criterion 3.3.1: Error Identification](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/error-identification.html).

## Submission

- Submit concise notes with:
- One selected primary user story and one set of acceptance criteria for that story.
- One draft critical workflow definition that includes trigger, main path, one alternate path, and success state.
- One draft sitemap for your project idea and one IA risk/uncertainty.
- One draft wireframe sketch for a core workflow with at least one accessibility annotation.
- Two questions you want to discuss in class.
