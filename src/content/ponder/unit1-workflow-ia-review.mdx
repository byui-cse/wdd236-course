## Introduction

This practice activity helps you pressure-test your Unit 1 planning artifacts before submitting the prove assignment.

Your goal is to find weak spots now, while changes are still cheap.

## **01** Workflow Quality Check

Review your critical workflow and verify all four parts are present:

1. Trigger
2. Main path
3. Alternate/error path
4. Success state

Then verify alignment with your planning artifacts:

1. Which persona does this workflow primarily serve?
2. Which user story does this workflow implement?
3. Do your acceptance criteria match the success state?

Use this quick test:

- Can another student read the workflow and explain it back to you accurately?
- Is the alternate path realistic (not a rare edge case)?
- Does the success state describe an observable outcome?

## **02** IA Quality Check

Review your sitemap with these prompts:

- Can users predict where each major action lives?
- Are labels clear and specific?
- Is any page overloaded with unrelated responsibilities?
- Where is the highest IA risk or uncertainty?

If needed, simplify your sitemap before adding more pages.

## **03** Wireframe Quality Check

For each wireframe screen/state, verify:

1. Primary action is visually obvious
2. Supporting content is grouped logically
3. Error or feedback areas are accounted for
4. One accessibility consideration is documented

Accessibility reminders:

- Ensure labels and instructions are explicit
- Plan where error messages appear and what they say
- Avoid relying only on color to communicate status

## **04** Team Discussion Prompts

Bring these to class or team discussion:

1. Which design decision currently has the weakest evidence?
2. Where might users get stuck in the workflow?
3. What is one simplification you can make this week?
4. Are any planned features outside MVP still showing up in your workflow or wireframes?

## Optional Stretch

Do a 5-minute peer walkthrough:

1. Share your workflow and wireframes with one classmate
2. Ask them to narrate what they think happens step by step
3. Capture one misunderstanding and revise your artifacts accordingly
