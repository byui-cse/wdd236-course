## Unit Focus (Weeks 9-13)

Unit 3 is the final repetition of the competency loop. You will scale from single components to a multi-view application, validate end-to-end behavior, and complete final mastery evidence.

### Competency Emphasis

- Competency 1: Architecture and system planning
- Competency 2: Component engineering and implementation
- Competency 3: Testing and quality automation
- Competency 4: State and data flow management
- Competency 5: Accessibility engineering
- Competency 6: AI-assisted technical review

### Weekly Flow

1. Week 9: System architecture and dependency planning (Comp 1 and 2)
2. Week 10: App shell and unified state (Comp 2 and 4)
3. Week 11: End-to-end workflow testing (Comp 3 and 6)
4. Week 12: App-wide accessibility and optimization (Comp 5)
5. Week 13: Technical lead audit, debt cleanup, and launch (Comp 6)

**Mastery checkpoint:** Final mastery assessment opportunity.
