## Unit Focus (Weeks 5-8)

Unit 2 repeats the full loop from Unit 1, but with external data streams and asynchronous complexity. You will design for unstable conditions, implement resilient async handling, and demonstrate mastery growth.

### Competency Emphasis

- Competency 1: Architecture and system planning
- Competency 2: Component engineering and implementation
- Competency 3: Testing and quality automation
- Competency 4: State and data flow management
- Competency 5: Accessibility engineering
- Competency 6: AI-assisted technical review

### Weekly Flow

1. Week 5: Data and flow architecture (Comp 1 and 4)
2. Week 6: Async component synthesis (Comp 2 and 4)
3. Week 7: Mock test automation for unstable systems (Comp 3 and 6)
4. Week 8: Dynamic accessibility and release gate (Comp 5 and 6)

**Mastery checkpoint:** Second complete mastery assessment opportunity.
