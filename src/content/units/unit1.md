## Unit Focus (Weeks 1-4)

Unit 1 establishes the baseline competency loop for the course. You will deconstruct interfaces, build a component, test core states, and complete your first mastery checkpoint.

### Competency Emphasis

- Competency 1: Architecture and system planning
- Competency 2: Component engineering and implementation
- Competency 3: Testing and quality automation
- Competency 4: State and data flow management
- Competency 5: Accessibility engineering
- Competency 6: AI-assisted technical review

### Weekly Flow

1. Week 1: Lifecycle framework and environment setup
2. Week 2: Blueprint and design-token planning (Comp 1 and 2)
3. Week 3: State logic, Shadow DOM, and unit testing (Comp 3 and 4)
4. Week 4: Accessibility pathing and AI senior review gate (Comp 5 and 6)

**Mastery checkpoint:** First complete mastery assessment opportunity.
